Applied and Interpretable Machine Learning Research Group

We build machine learning systems with industry, and we study what is actually happening inside them.

The group Centre for Industrial Software
SDU · Sønderborg Two commitments that pull in opposite directions, which is the point.

A system that works is not the same as a system you can account for.

Machine learning has become ordinary infrastructure. It reads scans, drafts documents, routes decisions and sits inside products that people depend on. What has not become ordinary is the ability to say, afterwards, what the system actually did and why anyone should have believed it.

We work on both halves of that problem at once. Applied means the systems are real: built with companies and clinics, deployed, measured, and maintained after the paper is written. Interpretable means we hold ourselves to explaining them, and to testing whether our explanations are worth anything.

The second half is the harder one. An explanation is a claim about a system, and a claim needs a way of being wrong. Most of our methodological work is about giving explanations that property.

Research Four lines They share one method: borrow a mature tool from another field, state the mapping, and name what would falsify it.
Interpretability

What a trained model is doing, and when to believe the answer

Counterfactual explanation, mechanistic analysis, and identifiability: the question of whether a model's report about itself is evidence of anything. We are as interested in the tests that retire an explanation as in the explanations themselves.

Representation learning

How a network comes to encode what it encodes

What architecture, optimisation, data and scale do to the structure a model ends up with, and whether a capability that can be decoded from a representation is actually used by the output or merely sitting there unused.

Imaging & biosignals

Medical images and physiological signals

Segmentation, detection and clinical evaluation, with the explanation treated as part of the result rather than a figure added at the end. Work on ECG, MRI and mammography, and on the benchmarking practices the field uses to judge itself.

Language & software

Language models, and the software they reshape

Retrieval and evaluation for models that must answer from a real document base, and the architectural decisions that AI systems quietly impose on the software around them.
